Common Exercises

The following are common exercises that we encourage our clients to do as a part of their daily exercise routine.

Burpee
Burpees are great, full body exercise to increase your stregth and edurance.Begin in a standing postion drop into a squat and extend your hands forward, kick your feet back and then forward again quickly, and then jump up from a squatted position.
Plank
Plants build your core strength. To perform a plank,get in a push up postion and rest your forearms on the floor. Hold the position as long as you can.
Mountain Climber
Mountain climbers are a good cardio execerise.Place your hands on the floor in a push up position,then bring one knee up to you chest and then switch as quickly as you can(as through you are climbing a mountain).
